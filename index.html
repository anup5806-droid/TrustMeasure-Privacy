TrustMeasure · Android app

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TrustMeasure accesses, uses, stores and shares information when you use the TrustMeasure Android application.

TrustMeasure is an augmented-reality measuring tool. The current version does not require you to create an account and does not operate a TrustMeasure account or measurement-sync server.

1. Information and device features used by TrustMeasure

Camera and augmented reality

TrustMeasure requests camera access so it can show the live camera view, recognise surfaces, track the phone's position and orientation, and place measurement points and visual guides in the real-world view. The camera view is processed for the measuring session. The TrustMeasure app does not upload the live camera feed or saved measurement history to a TrustMeasure-operated server.

TrustMeasure uses Google Play Services for AR (ARCore), provided by Google. Google's ARCore guidance states that ARCore automatically collects Google account identifiers when a user is signed in to a Google account, or device-specific identifiers when the user is not signed in. ARCore also collects performance and diagnostic information about the app, ARCore SDK and device, and information about ARCore API calls and app activity. Google uses this information for ARCore analytics, diagnostics and service improvement. Google's handling of this information is governed by Google's own privacy information.

The current TrustMeasure version does not use the ARCore Geospatial API or Cloud Anchors. Those ARCore features can involve additional processing, such as precise location or video data, when an app uses them.

Learn more: ARCore data safety information, ARCore privacy requirements and Google Privacy Policy.

Motion sensors, flashlight and vibration

The Level tool reads the device's gravity sensor, or the accelerometer when a gravity sensor is unavailable, to calculate the phone's tilt. These sensor readings are used by the app on the device while the feature is active. TrustMeasure also uses the device camera and AR tracking information to position measurements. The flashlight can be switched on by the user for additional light, and vibration is used for tactile feedback.

Permissions

The current Android app requests camera, flashlight and vibration permissions or capabilities. It does not request location, microphone or contacts permissions.

2. Measurements and settings stored on your device

When you save a project or measurement, TrustMeasure stores it in the app's private data area on your device. A saved measurement can include:

  • the title you give the measurement;
  • the measurement mode, result and unit;
  • optional secondary measurement details; and
  • the date and time associated with the saved measurement.

Calibration values and app preferences are also stored locally. TrustMeasure uses Android's app-private storage for this information; it is not displayed to other ordinary apps through TrustMeasure.

The Android application currently allows system backup. Depending on your Android version, device settings and backup configuration, eligible app data may be included in an Android device backup or device transfer. Those backup services are controlled by Android and your device or Google account settings, not by a TrustMeasure-operated server.

3. Photos, PDF reports and sharing

Measurement snapshots

If you tap the camera shutter button in TrustMeasure, the app captures the current app screen. The snapshot can include the live camera view, your surroundings, measurement points, lines, labels and other visible screen information. The image is saved to your device's Pictures/TrustMeasure gallery folder. TrustMeasure does not send that snapshot to a TrustMeasure server.

PDF reports and measurement sharing

When you choose to create a PDF report, TrustMeasure generates the report temporarily in the app's cache. When you choose Share, Android displays available sharing apps so you can select where to send the report. You can also choose to share measurement text from a saved project. TrustMeasure does not send a report or measurement to another person unless you initiate a sharing action.

Files saved to your gallery remain there until you delete them. Temporary cache files may be removed by Android or when the app's cache is cleared. Once you share a file or message, copies held by the receiving app or recipient are subject to their own privacy practices and retention settings.

4. How information is shared

TrustMeasure does not sell your measurement history or photos. The app does not provide user accounts, an advertising service or its own analytics service in the current version.

Information may be processed by or shared with the following services when relevant:

  • Google Play Services for AR (ARCore): Google processes the ARCore identifiers, performance and diagnostic information, and API-usage information described above. See Google's policy links in Section 1.
  • Android backup services: eligible app data may be backed up or transferred according to your device and account settings.
  • Apps you choose for sharing: when you share a report, image or measurement, the selected app and recipient receive the content you choose to send.

TrustMeasure does not operate a cloud account, cloud measurement library or service for uploading your saved measurement projects in the current version.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Saved measurements: delete an individual entry from the Projects list in TrustMeasure.
  • Calibration and app preferences: these remain in the app's local data until you clear TrustMeasure's app storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app. Clearing app storage also removes locally saved measurement history.
  • Snapshots: delete exported images from your device's Gallery or photo app.
  • PDF reports: temporary report files are stored in the app cache and may be removed by Android or when the cache is cleared. Delete any copies you shared from the receiving app or destination as well.
  • Device backups and shared copies: deleting data from TrustMeasure does not necessarily delete an earlier device backup or copies already shared with another app or person. Manage those copies through the relevant device, backup or receiving service.

TrustMeasure does not maintain a user account or a TrustMeasure server containing saved measurement history that you need to request us to delete.

6. Security

Saved measurements and preferences are kept in Android app-private storage. Access to that storage is subject to Android's operating-system protections and the security of your device. Google states that data collected by ARCore is encrypted in transit on supported devices. No app, device or method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

We recommend keeping your device and Android software up to date and using an appropriate screen lock.

7. Third-party services

TrustMeasure relies on Google Play Services for AR (ARCore) for augmented-reality functionality. Use of Google services is also governed by Google's applicable terms and privacy information. Android backup services and apps you choose for sharing are separate services and have their own policies.

8. Changes to this Privacy Policy
